Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

JNEET+ AI is free to use at launch. This page explains how payments, refunds and cancellation will work once paid plans are available. No payment is taken until a paid plan is clearly shown to you at checkout.

1. Free plan

The free plan costs nothing and has no refund because nothing is charged.

2. Paid plans (when available)

  • Paid plans are sold as prepaid passes for a fixed period. They do not renew automatically unless the checkout page clearly says so and you agreed to it.
  • Prices are shown in Indian rupees, inclusive of applicable taxes, before you pay.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

3. Refunds

All purchases are final: paid plans are non-refundable once purchased, and we do not give refunds for unused time or because you changed your mind. Please try the free plan first to see if JNEET+ AI suits you.

  • The only exceptions are our own errors: if you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or paid and your plan was never activated because of a fault on our side, we will correct it or refund the affected amount in full.
  • Nothing here limits any right you have under Indian consumer law that cannot be waived.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and normally reach you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

4. Cancelling

If a plan is set to renew, you can turn renewal off any time before the renewal date and keep access until the paid period ends.

5. How to ask

Email official.krishupadhyay@gmail.com from your registered address with the payment reference. We reply within 2 working days.
