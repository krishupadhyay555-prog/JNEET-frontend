Delivery Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

JNEET+ AI is an online service. Nothing is shipped physically. This page explains how and when you get access after you sign up or buy a plan.

1. What you receive

Access to the JNEET+ AI website (AI mentor, mock tests, revision, OneShot notes and progress tracking) on your account. There are no physical goods, so there is no shipping, shipping charge or delivery address.

2. When you get access

  • Free plan: immediately after you verify your email.
  • Paid plans (Pro / Elite): automatically, as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment — usually within a few seconds. Your plan, its end date and a receipt email confirm it.
  • If your plan is not active within 30 minutes of a successful payment, contact us with the payment ID. Our system also re-checks payments automatically every few minutes. If we cannot activate it, you get a full refund (see the Refund & Cancellation Policy).

3. Where it works

On any modern web browser on phone, tablet or computer, at jneetai.com, in India. You need an internet connection; some notes also work offline once opened.
