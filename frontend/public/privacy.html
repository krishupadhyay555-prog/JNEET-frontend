Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

JNEET+ AI ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered exam-preparation platform for NEET and JEE students in India. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account and use JNEET+ AI, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Exam preference — whether you're preparing for NEET or JEE, and your target exam year.
  • Chat content — questions you ask our AI Mentor and the AI's responses, so you can revisit past conversations.
  • Test and revision activity — your answers, scores, and accuracy on practice tests and revision sessions, used to power the Weakness Management System (WMS).
  • Uploaded images — if you upload a photo of a question or diagram for the AI to read.
  • Technical information — basic device and browser information for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the AI Mentor chat, practice tests, and weakness-tracking features.
  • To personalize your experience — for example, showing your weak/medium/strong topics.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent misuse.
  • To improve the app based on how it's used.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-party services we use

To provide our service, we share limited data with these trusted providers:

  • Google Gemini API — processes the questions you send to the AI Mentor, in order to generate a response.
  • MongoDB Atlas — securely stores your account and activity data.
  • Cloudinary — stores images you upload (such as question photos).
  • Razorpay — if you choose to purchase a paid plan, Razorpay processes your payment securely. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — Razorpay handles this in compliance with RBI and PCI-DSS payment security standards. We only receive confirmation that a payment was completed.

4. Cookies and sessions

We use a secure, httpOnly authentication cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie cannot be read by JavaScript and is used only to verify your identity with our servers — not for advertising or tracking across other websites.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, you can contact us using the details below.

6. Your rights

  • You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • You can request correction of inaccurate data.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

7. Children's privacy

JNEET+ AI is intended for students preparing for NEET and JEE, who are typically teenagers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without appropriate parental context. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
