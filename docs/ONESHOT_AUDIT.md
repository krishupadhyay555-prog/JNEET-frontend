# OneShot audit — NEET 2025 + JEE Main 2026 (19 Nov 2026)

Question asked by the founder: if a student learns ONLY the OneShot notes very well, can they qualify?
Method: every question of two real NTA papers (NEET (UG) 2025, 180 Q; JEE Main 2026 · 22 Jan · Shift 1, 75 Q) was opened
as NTA's image and judged against that chapter's OneShot by helpers:
A = direct recall / one step · B = everything is in the OneShot but solving needs practice · C = a needed fact is missing · X = dropped / several answers.

| Part | A | B | C | X |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NEET 2025 · Physics | 19 | 22 | 3 | 1 |
| NEET 2025 · Chemistry | 21 | 11 | 12 | 1 |
| NEET 2025 · Biology Q91–135 | 28 | 9 | 8 | 0 |
| NEET 2025 · Biology Q136–180 | 31 | 5 | 9 | 0 |
| JEE Main 2026 (22 Jan S1) · Maths | 6 | 16 | 3 | 0 |
| JEE Main 2026 (22 Jan S1) · Physics + Chemistry | 11 | 28 | 10 | 1 |

Totals: **NEET 99 A (55%) · 47 B (26%) · 32 C (18%) · 2 X; JEE 17 A (23%) · 44 B (59%) · 13 C (17%) · 1 X.**
(NEET Chemistry Q65/Q69/Q82/Q85 were first matched to the wrong OneShot — Physics thermodynamics / Biology biomolecules — and are counted against the right file.)

Reading (estimate, before this PR's fixes): a OneShot-only NEET student scores ~320–490/720 — above the 2025 general qualifying mark (144) —
but not enough for a government MBBS seat. A OneShot-only JEE student scores ~60–135/300: borderline for the JEE Advanced cutoff
(93.41 percentile in 2026). JEE needs problem practice (59% of questions are B), which mocks, PYQs and the Mistake Notebook give.

This PR fixes every C item and every "missing" note the helpers listed (69 items, 43 chapters, English + Hinglish) and three
misleading lines (frog respiration on land, N vs Cl electronegativity, closest-approach vs diameter trap in Atoms).
Next: repeat on 2–3 more papers after launch.
